# IITM TDS GA3 face deliverables

- `jonathan-winters.glb`: a self-contained textured 3D head model reconstructed from the assigned photograph; approximately 316 KB.
- `face-morph.mp4`: a four-second landmark-based morph between the assigned Charles Michel and Sofia Coppola photographs; 256 × 256, 24 fps, approximately 85 KB.

These are assignment artifacts. The morph is an artificial transformation, not footage of a real event.

## Photograph credits

- Jonathan Winters photograph: cropped U.S. Department of Defense photograph, unknown author, public domain. [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jonathan-Winters_1986.JPEG). Changes: face geometry reconstruction, head completion and texture projection.
- Charles Michel photograph: Unión Europea en Perú, [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Charles_Michel_(49467991288).jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/).
- Sofia Coppola photograph: Raph_PH, [Wikimedia Commons source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SofiaCoppPicCen081223_(6_of_22)_(53388050173)_(cropped).jpg), [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). The Commons photograph is itself a crop of the original Flickr photograph.

Morph changes: cropping, eye/mouth alignment, landmark-based geometric warping, intermediate frame blending, resizing and video compression. The resulting morph video is shared under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). No source photographer endorses these derived artifacts.
